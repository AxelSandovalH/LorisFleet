01WHAT TIME DOES THE CHARTER DEPART?

We leave at 7:00 AM from Fisherman's Wharf. The 4-hour bay trips depart at 8:00 AM. We return according to the trip length you booked, or once you have your catch.

02HOW MANY PEOPLE CAN FISH?

The Lori III and Lori II carry up to 8 people; the Five Reasons up to 4. Mexican regulations require a fishing license for every passenger 12 and older, whether they fish or not.

03IS EQUIPMENT INCLUDED?

Yes. Rods, reels, terminal tackle and bait are included on every trip. You are welcome to bring your own gear.

04WHAT DO YOU CATCH AND WHEN?

Sailfish almost year-round; blue and black marlin, mahi-mahi and yellowfin tuna peak from October to March. Inside the bay: jack crevalle, sierra, skipjack and snapper.

05CAN I KEEP MY CATCH?

Yes, as long as it is legal. The crew cleans it at the end of the trip. Sailfish and marlin are released unless they come up injured.

06WHAT IS THE CANCELLATION POLICY?

If the captain cancels due to weather, you get a full refund or a new date. For customer cancellations, ask us when booking.
