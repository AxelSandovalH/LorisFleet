ANOTHER QUESTION?

How do I book?

Choose a date, boat and trip with the Book button. We show the total instantly and the request reaches us on WhatsApp. Nothing is charged when you send it: the captain confirms availability and explains how to secure your date.

What time does the charter leave?

We depart at 7:00 AM from the Fishermen's Pier. The 4-hour bay tours leave at 8:00 AM. We return according to the length you booked, or earlier once you have your catch.

How much does it cost and what is included?

Rates are per boat, not per person, so you can split the cost with your group. They include captain, crew, tackle and bait. See the rates section for each boat and trip length.

What is not included in the price?

The Mexican fishing license (required from age 12), food and drinks (except on "all inclusive" trips), a tip for the crew (15 to 20% is recommended) and transportation to the pier.

What is the cancellation policy?

If the captain cancels due to bad weather, you get a full refund or a new date. For guest cancellations, ask us when you book.

How do I get to the pier?

We leave from the Fishermen's Pier in Manzanillo, Colima. The contact section has the map and a button that opens directions on your phone.

How many people can fish?

The Lori III and Lori II carry up to 8 people; the Five Reasons up to 4. Mexican regulations require a fishing license for every passenger aged 12 or older, whether they fish or not.

Is the equipment included?

Yes. Rods, reels, terminal tackle and bait are included on every trip. You are welcome to bring your own gear.

Which boat is right for me?

The Lori III is the flagship, ideal for running up to 30 miles offshore after marlin and big tuna. The Lori II has plenty of room for groups and families. The Five Reasons is fast and agile, for groups of up to four.

Can I bring children?

Yes. The captain adapts the trip to your group, and the Lori II offers the most room for families. If you are traveling with small children, tell us when you book so we can recommend the right trip and boat.

Is there a bathroom on board?

Yes. The Lori III and Lori II have a bathroom and cooler; the Five Reasons has a bathroom, beds, cooler and snorkel gear.

What should I bring?

Sunscreen, a hat, polarized sunglasses and light clothing. Soft-soled non-slip shoes and your fishing license. If you get seasick easily, take your medication before departure.

What can I catch and when?

Sailfish almost all year; blue and black marlin, mahi-mahi and yellowfin tuna are strongest from October to March. Inside the bay: jack, sierra, bonito and snapper.

Can I keep my catch?

Yes, as long as it is legal. The crew cleans it at the end of the trip. Sailfish and marlin are released unless they are injured.
